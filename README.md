# Puffy Guide 

**Experience Chiang Mai Locally, Verify Globally.**

Puffy Guide is an interactive travel companion designed to connect tourists with local businesses and cultural experiences in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

Our project combines a cute physical mascot concept with a digital travel experience, helping visitors discover local attractions, explore destinations, complete quests, and engage with local rewards.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Discover Chiang Mai:** Explore local attractions and cultural destinations.
- **Interactive Travel Experience:** Navigate travel-related content through a user-friendly interface.
- **Quests and Rewards:** Encourage exploration through gamification and digital rewards.
- **AI Travel Assistance:** Explore an AI-powered travel companion concept.
- **Blockchain & Digital Collectibles:** Demonstrate the potential of blockchain technology for digital collectibles.
